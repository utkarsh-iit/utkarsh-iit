Biography of Utkarsh Roy

I am an Electrical Engineer and Technical Assistant at the Department of Electrical Engineering, Indian Institute of Technology Delhi, where I support laboratory operations, technical education, and research activities. Alongside my professional responsibilities, I am pursuing a part-time Ph.D. focusing on Battery Management Systems, Embedded Systems, Edge AI, and Optical Communication.

My technical expertise includes embedded systems development, FPGA design, Internet of Things (IoT), machine learning for edge devices, PCB design, rapid prototyping, and laboratory infrastructure management. I have experience working with STM32, ESP32, RP2040, Nordic nRF52, Raspberry Pi, NVIDIA Jetson, and various FPGA platforms.

I am passionate about building reliable embedded systems that combine hardware, software, and artificial intelligence to solve real-world engineering challenges. My long-term goal is to contribute to research and innovation in intelligent embedded systems while fostering practical engineering education through laboratory development and student mentorship.

I enjoy transforming research ideas into practical engineering solutions. Whether it's designing embedded hardware, deploying machine learning models on microcontrollers, developing battery management algorithms, or mentoring students in the laboratory, I'm always eager to learn, build, and collaborate. If you're interested in embedded systems, Edge AI, battery technologies, or collaborative research, I'd be happy to connect.
