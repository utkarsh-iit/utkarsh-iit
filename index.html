Introduction

Utkarsh Roy holds a B.Tech in Electrical Engineering from Bengal College of Engineering and Technology, Durgapur, affiliated to Maulana Abul Kalam Azad University of Technology, West Bengal (2014–2018).

He then worked as Graduate Engineer Trainee at EMC Limited, in railway electrification projects. He did the HT-LT Transformation along with the laying of catanary and contact wire. In July 2023, he joined the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi as a PhD Scholar in Embedded Systems Designing under the supervision of Prof. Subrat Kar, focusing on battery management systems algorithm design. He is currently serving as Technical Assistant in the Embedded Systems Lab, Department of Electrical Engineering, IIT Delhi since May 2020.
